Satar · Privacy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. Satar is made by Elghaly LLC. Questions: support@elghaly.dev.

Data we collect: none. Satar does not collect, share or sell personal data. Everything listed below stays on your phone unless you choose to send it.

Satar has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page lists what the app keeps on your phone, what can leave it, and what we cannot see or control.

What stays on your phone

  • Checking a forward. The text you paste is checked inside the page, on your phone. Satar does not send it anywhere.
  • Stored in your browser, for this site only: your notes, your language choice, the lock (a hash of your passcode, not the passcode), the place you chose or found by GPS for prayer times (latitude, longitude, time zone), songs you add or keep, and, if you turn on Face ID, an identifier your device gives Satar (never your face or fingerprint). Your cash notebook entries are kept here too.
  • GPS is used only when you tap for it, to compute prayer times on the phone. The place is kept on the phone as above.
  • Prayer times or prayer hours. Whether you chose Muslim prayer times or Christian prayer hours (Agpeya), and whether you turned on fixed clock times, is kept on this phone only and never sent anywhere. The times are computed on the phone from the place you chose.
  • Prayer reminders are off until you turn them on. Your phone asks you first; reminders are shown by your phone while Satar is open, and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Camera (mirror) is used only when you tap it. Nothing is recorded or sent; the camera turns off when you close the mirror or leave the page.
  • Camera (code check) is used only when you tap "Scan with camera"; Satar asks for camera permission at that moment. The picture is read on your phone to find a QR code or barcode (QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, PDF417, EAN, UPC, Code 128/39, ITF); nothing is recorded, saved or sent, and the camera turns off when a code is found, when you tap "Stop camera" or when you leave the page. If you say no, "Scan from a photo" still works: the photo you pick is read on your phone and is not uploaded or kept.
  • What a code says is checked on your phone: by the same check as a forwarded message, and for addresses that imitate Google or YouTube, hide their real address, or use short links. Nothing is sent anywhere for this. A link opens only if you tap "Open link" after the result. A Wi-Fi code's password stays hidden until you tap to show it.
  • Code maker. The text, link, Wi-Fi details, contact, phone number or email you type is turned into a QR code on your phone. It is not uploaded and not saved.
  • To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What can leave your phone, and only when you choose

  • Backup. Satar makes a backup file on your phone. It includes your notes, your cash notebook, your language and your saved place. It leaves the phone only if you send it yourself, with your own mail or SMS app.
  • Radio. "Sunday Morning" loads a YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com), only after you tap it. From then on, YouTube's own privacy policy applies. What the player does after it loads is outside Satar's control.
  • Music (public-domain melodies) plays or downloads from this same site when you tap it.
  • News. "Sky News Arabia" loads that channel's live YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com), only after you tap it. From then on, YouTube's own privacy policy applies. What the player does after it loads is outside Satar's control.
  • Live TV (Islamic editions). "Holy Quran" and "Al-Azhar" load that channel's live YouTube player (youtube-nocookie.com), only after you tap it. Only one player is shown at a time. From then on, YouTube's own privacy policy applies. What the player does after it loads is outside Satar's control. A channel that is not set up yet loads nothing.
  • Reading aloud ("Hear verdict", the Adhan voice) uses your phone's speech feature. On some phones that feature may use an online service; that depends on your phone, not on Satar.
  • Opening the site. The web host that serves these pages may keep standard request logs (such as your IP address and the page asked for), as any website host does.
