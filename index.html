Satar

Check a forward before you trust it.

Made for your phone. Some features use the internet.

It's yours, for you — for the people 💜

What it does

  • Checking a forward. The text you paste is checked inside the page, on your phone. Satar does not send it anywhere.
  • Code check. A QR code or barcode is read on your phone, and checked for addresses that imitate Google or YouTube, hide their real address, or use short links. A link opens only if you tap "Open link".
  • No accounts, no ads and no analytics. Data we collect: none.
